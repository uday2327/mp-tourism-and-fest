Khajuraho

Khajuraho Dance Festival

Khajuraho Dance Festival is a famous cultural festival held at Khajuraho Temples in Madhya Pradesh. It is organized by the Madhya Pradesh Kala Parishad to promote Indian classical dance. The festival began in the 1970s to highlight India’s dance heritage. Dancers perform styles like Bharatanatyam, Kathak, Odissi, and Kuchipudi. Performances take place in front of the beautifully lit temples. Its significance is in preserving classical dance and promoting tourism.

Gwalior

Tansen Music Festival

Tansen Samaroh (Tansen Music Utsav) is a famous classical music festival held in Gwalior, Madhya Pradesh. It is organized to honor the great musician Miyan Tansen, one of the nine gems of Akbar’s court. The festival has a long history and celebrates India’s classical music tradition. Top classical singers and musicians perform near Tansen’s tomb. Its significance is in preserving and promoting Hindustani classical music.

Indore

Malwa Utsav

Malwa Utsav is a cultural festival of the Malwa region of Madhya Pradesh that celebrates Malwa’s folk culture. It is organized by the state tourism and cultural departments with local artists. The festival started to promote and preserve Malwa’s traditional arts and heritage. It includes folk dances, music, handicraft fairs, and local food stalls. Artists perform in traditional dress with live folk instruments. Its significance is in protecting regional culture and attracting tourism.

BundelKhand

BundelKhand Rai Utsav

Bundelkhand Rai Utsav is a folk cultural celebration from the Bundelkhand region of Madhya Pradesh based on the traditional Rai dance. It originated in villages where music and dance were part of social celebrations. The festival features fast Rai dance performances with dholak and folk songs. Dancers wear colorful traditional dress and perform in fairs or cultural events. It is significant for preserving Bundelkhand’s culture and supporting local artists.

Jhabua

Bhagoria Festival

Bhagoria Festival is a vibrant tribal festival of Madhya Pradesh, mainly celebrated in the Jhabua–Alirajpur region. It is associated with the Bhil and Bhilala tribes and takes place before Holi. The festival is known for music, dance, colorful markets, and traditional dress. Traditionally, it was also a time when young people chose their life partners. Bhagoria reflects the rich tribal culture and joyful spirit of the region.

Bhopal

Lokrang Festival

Lokrang Festival is a famous cultural festival celebrated in Madhya Pradesh, usually held in Bhopal around Republic Day. It showcases India’s rich folk dances, music, and traditional art forms. Artists from many states perform to promote cultural unity. Handicrafts, local food, and tribal traditions are also highlighted. The festival helps preserve and celebrate India’s folk heritage.

Region-wise Festival Guide

Use this area to expand festival content by region. Keep the notes short and add or edit items as you collect more details.

Malwa (Indore, Ujjain)

Malwa Utsav, Rangpanchami in Indore, Ujjain temple celebrations, and food fairs during festive weeks. Expect folk dance, food streets, and craft bazaars.

Bundelkhand (Orchha, Datia)

Orchha festival evenings, local fairs near Datia, and temple events in small towns. Combine with forts, river ghats, and heritage walks.

Baghelkhand (Rewa, Satna)

Seasonal village fairs, devotional music nights, and craft bazaars around Rewa and Satna. Great for quiet, cultural experiences.

Mahakoshal (Jabalpur)

Festivals near the Narmada, local dance performances, and craft markets. Pair with Bhedaghat and Dhuandhar waterfall visits.

Nimar (Maheshwar, Mandu)

Textile traditions in Maheshwar, riverside festivals, and heritage celebrations near Mandu. Look for weaving demos and craft stalls.

Tribal Belt (Jhabua, Alirajpur)

Bhagoria fair, folk music circles, and vibrant weekly markets. Expect color, dance, and community celebrations in the western belt.
