History & Narmada

Madhya Pradesh has a deep, layered history of kingdoms, art, and culture that shaped central India.

The sacred Narmada River flows through the heart of the state, lovingly called Narmada Maiya by devotees.

Rivers of Madhya Pradesh

Life lines that shape the state’s culture, farms, and towns.

Narmada: Central India’s sacred river, flowing west and nurturing historic towns and ghats.

Chambal: Known for dramatic ravines and wildlife sanctuaries along its clean, scenic waters.

Shipra: Ujjain’s holy river, central to rituals and the grand Kumbh Mela festival.

Betwa: Flows past Orchha’s palaces and cenotaphs, adding charm to Bundelkhand heritage.

Ken: The lifeline of Panna region, known for scenic gorges and the Ken Gharial Sanctuary.

Son: A major eastern tributary, supporting farms and settlements across northern MP.

More About Madhya Pradesh

A balanced blend of heritage cities, tribal culture, and natural beauty.

Geography: From the Vindhyas to Satpura ranges, MP holds hills, forests, and fertile plains.

Culture: Folk dances like Rai and Matki, crafts like Gond art, and vibrant fairs keep traditions alive.

Food: Famous for poha-jalebi, dal bafla, and spicy street foods in Indore, Bhopal, and Ujjain.

Wildlife: Tiger reserves such as Kanha, Bandhavgarh, and Pench make it a top safari destination.
